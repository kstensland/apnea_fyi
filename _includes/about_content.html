Emerald City Apnea Challenge (ECAC) is a community-run freediving organization based in Seattle, Washington. We host AIDA freediving competitions and events to support the growing freediving community across the Pacific Northwest.

We hold our pool competitions at Snohomish Aquatic Center in Snohomish, WA. We've previously hosted depth competitions in the Puget Sound and Lake Washington. In 2027, we aim to host a multi-day depth competition in Lake Crescent on the Olympic Peninsula.

Our competitions follow AIDA freediving rules. Find upcoming competitions and events in the Events section, and follow us on the social media links at the bottom of every page.
