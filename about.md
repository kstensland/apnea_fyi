Emerald City Apnea Challenge is a freediving organization that hosts competitions for athletes and events for volunteers in the Pacific Northwest.

Our competitions follow AIDA freediving rules. Find upcoming competitions and events on the [Events](/events/) page, and follow us on the social media links at the bottom of every page.

Questions? Email us at [emeraldcityapneachallenge@gmail.com](mailto:emeraldcityapneachallenge@gmail.com).
