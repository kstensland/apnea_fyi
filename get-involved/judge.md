If you would like to judge at one of our competitions, please email us your AIDA judge profile. If there is a specific competition you’d like to judge at, please include that in the email as well.

We prioritize our local judges, higher level judges, and judges who need to judge to keep their active status.

Questions? Email us at <a href="mailto:emeraldcityapneachallenge@gmail.com">emeraldcityapneachallenge@gmail.com</a>.
